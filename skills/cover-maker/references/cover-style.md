# Cover Style

## Core Grammar

- support two default deliverables:
  - vertical poster composition `3:4`
  - horizontal commentary cover `4:3`
- one dominant face, body, or scene
- oversized Chinese headline at the top
- strong verdict text at the bottom
- top headline and bottom verdict should fill their bands horizontally; do not leave large unused blocks
- subtitle/tag text should be visible enough to read at thumbnail size
- thick cream, gold, orange, or blue border
- dark cinematic grading with visible contrast
- grain, worn print texture, and slight shadow help the cover feel less clean
- final cover text should be generated through `imagegen` by default; do not assume a local default font unless the user explicitly asks for a manual text-overlay fallback

## Text Hierarchy

Top title:
- preserve an explicit user title verbatim; the 4-8 character target applies only when creating a new title
- category or verdict
- examples: `学历幻觉`, `大学真相`, `身份定价`, `专业骗局`
- should visually span most of the width, especially in `4:3`

Bottom conclusion:
- 2-4 lines
- 10-22 characters total when possible
- should sound like the payoff of the script
- examples: `读了四年 / 才发现市场 / 没等你`
- usually highlight 1-3 key words with a stronger color such as orange or gold
- for structural critique topics, highlight the payoff words rather than the setup words
- should fill the lower band, not sit as a small left-side block

## Common Looks

Black Orange Figure:
- default for famous/public figure covers
- black-and-white or desaturated real person photo
- vivid orange border
- cream/gold top title with heavy black stroke
- white bottom verdict with orange emphasis words
- dense full-frame layout; avoid empty right side or bottom side
- keep the figure recognizable even after grading and texture

Black Gold Authority:
- dark suit, old office, classroom shadow, institution mood
- cream or faded-gold type
- warm beige border

Orange Heat Hustle:
- black/orange/rust palette
- intense face or crowded scene
- aggressive urgency

Blue Impact Epic:
- deep blue/cyan palette
- one person against a large force
- best for AI/future/system shocks
